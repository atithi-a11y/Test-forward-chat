Before you start

Please keep these points in mind when you use this chat.

  • What this chat coversSchool attendance and 3rd grade reading scores only.
  • Public school students onlyThe data does not include all children in Brown County. Students in private schools, homeschool, and other settings are not represented.
  • Not every student or circumstance is shownThese numbers cannot tell the full story of any child, family, or school. They show what happened, not why.
  • AI can be wrongAnswers may contain mistakes. Please double-check important figures in WISEdash.
